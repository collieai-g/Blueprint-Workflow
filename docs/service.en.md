## Build a method that remains useful

Blueprint Workflow records, understands and develops reusable methods while you work with AI. It preserves important instructions, decisions, corrections and artifact references so the method can be continued, handed over and verified with new inputs.

Blueprint Studio is planning to offer the product as a **monthly service**. The formal offering will define:

| Area | What the formal offering will explain |
| --- | --- |
| Product access | Eligibility, access methods and availability. |
| Installation and integration | Supported local environments, AI hosts, analysis engines and setup arrangements. |
| Updates and maintenance | Update delivery, version management and data continuity arrangements. |
| Usage support | Support scope, delivery method and applicable conditions. |
| Pricing | Monthly fees, service coverage and associated conditions. |

Plans, pricing, support commitments and a launch date have not been announced. This repository does not provide payment, subscription activation or software downloads.
